This sample renders a pivot with `Region → City` on the row axis and
`Department × Measure` on the column axis. Aggregation happens in a small in-memory
shaping helper — no SQL, no DuckDB — and the result is handed to the renderer as a
`PivotDataViewModel`.

<SampleDemo id="sales-pivot" height={420} />

## How it works

- Distinct `(region, city)` tuples become the two row facet levels; distinct
  `department` values crossed with the two measures become the column facet levels.
- The data is column-major: `data[col][row]`, with `null` where a combination has no
  source rows.
- The grid uses the `pivot` layout (`StandardLayout`) and renders multi-level facet
  headers directly from the facet arrays.
