---
title: Samples
description: Self-contained grid samples built on the DataViewModel to Renderer bridge
---

Each sample is a self-contained directory in `packages/samples` — the page you are
reading, the executable code, the conversation that produced it, and its dataset
references all live together. Samples construct ViewModels directly in memory, so
they exercise the DataViewModel ↔ Renderer bridge without a DataSource or DataModel.
