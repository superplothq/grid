⏺ Here's the difference:

  - getData — the low-level abstract method. Returns raw { rowData, 
  totalRowCount }. You then manually construct the FlattenedDataViewModel
  yourself.
  - getViewModelData — the higher-level entry point. Internally calls getData,
  but also handles page caching, eviction, metadata plumbing, and returns
  FlattenedDataViewModelParams ready to pass directly into new 
  FlattenedDataViewModel(...).

  For our sample, getViewModelData is the right one — it handles pagination (the
   grid can fetch more pages as you scroll), which matters with 50K rows.
